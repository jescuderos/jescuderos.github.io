Teaching

Teaching in computer architecture, networks, practical systems and postgraduate education.

I teach at the School of Computer Science and Engineering (ESII), University of Castilla-La Mancha, in Albacete. Since 2006, I have taught 15 university courses, mainly in Computer Architecture and Technology, at undergraduate and postgraduate level, including more than 200 hours in English within UCLM’s bilingual Computer Science degree and teaching experience at the Universitat Politècnica de València and Heidelberg University. My teaching was rated Very Favourable by the DOCENTIA-UCLM programme in 2021–2022 and 2022–2023, and is closely connected to my supervision of undergraduate, master’s and doctoral research projects in computer architecture and interconnection networks.

Postgraduate education

I co-direct UCLM’s postgraduate Master’s Programme in Microelectronic Systems Based on Open Architectures, developed within the DMA2 Chair. The programme combines architecture, open hardware and applied microelectronic-system design with contributions from academic and industrial specialists.

My previous postgraduate teaching includes Advanced Seminars, Management of Information Technologies and Innovation, High-Performance Networks, and invited master’s teaching on high-performance interconnection networks at Heidelberg University.

Practical learning and teaching innovation

Hands-on learning is central to my teaching. I organized five editions of Hw-Lab, a 20-hour Raspberry Pi workshop in which students designed and developed complete hardware/software systems.

I have co-directed teaching-innovation projects on:

  • Integrating the open RISC-V architecture across Computer Engineering courses.
  • Coordinating and updating the Computer Engineering specialization at UCLM.

I have also contributed to project-based learning and bilingual-teaching initiatives and co-authored peer-reviewed work on computer-engineering education.

International teaching and outreach

In 2017, I directed the first international edition of UCLM’s Computer Science Summer School: High-Performance Interconnection Networks for HPC and Data Centers towards the Exascale and Big-Data Era. The programme brought international speakers and students to Albacete for advanced training in interconnection-network research.

I have also delivered invited seminars and courses at Heidelberg University, the University of Murcia and an academic institution in Peru, as well as technical workshops for UCLM students.
