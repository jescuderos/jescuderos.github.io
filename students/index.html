Students

PhD supervision and selected undergraduate and master's research projects.

I supervise student research on high-performance interconnection networks, routing, congestion management, network architecture and hardware systems. This work often connects teaching with ongoing research: several students first contributed through an undergraduate or master’s project before continuing towards a PhD.
